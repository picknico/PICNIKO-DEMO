# V31.5 Creative AI + Media

Adds an additive creative layer to Digital Marketing:
- image/video selection with local preview
- private `ad-creatives` Supabase Storage bucket
- campaign description, hashtags, ad keywords and AI strategy metadata
- PIKO AI Boost draft generation for headline, primary text, CTA, hashtags and keyword cluster
- no automatic publishing; user reviews before campaign creation
- no private messages or exact private location used as ad targeting inputs
